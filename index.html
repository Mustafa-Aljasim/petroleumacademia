Petroleum Academia is a learning platform for people who want to understand petroleum engineering beyond the equations.

We share educational resources, courses, and practical tools with a focus on reservoir engineering. Our approach brings concepts, data interpretation, and engineering applications together to make technical learning clear and useful.
